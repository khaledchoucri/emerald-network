## Purpose and visual brief

- **Intended use and player-facing purpose.** This is the style reference for every later battle asset. Khaled picked
  "hi-bit" art (twice Emerald's pixel detail) on the vision page. This frame decides what that means for us: line
  weight, palette, panel material, lighting and effect look. It will not ship in the game.
- **Deliverable type:** concept.
- **Subject and composition:** one grid-battle moment, seen the way the game shows it: a straight-on view, slightly from
  above.
  - Follow the geometry in `references/hibit_battle_layout_528x352.png`:
    - a 6×3 grid of panels across the lower half;
    - the left 3 columns have red frames (player), the right 3 blue frames (foe);
    - the bottom row shows a front edge.
  - Above the grid is the "Net" sky: a dark digital space with a faint receding grid and data motes.
  - One fighter stands on panel (1,1) and one on (4,1).
  - Show a mid-flight attack: a fireball travelling from the player's side towards the foe, with a small impact burst on
    one blue panel.
  - Mix the panel types: most normal, plus one cracked panel, one grass panel and one ice panel.
  - Leave the HUD zones from the layout guide visually quiet: HP boxes top-left and top-right, the gauge top-centre, and
    the text line at the bottom. Draw empty frames there and no text.
- **Fighters:** original placeholders only.
  - Use simple glowing data-form shapes: a rounded capsule body with a visor-like eye band, about one panel wide and 1.3
    panels tall.
  - Player placeholder: warm orange. Foe placeholder: cool teal.
  - They must not resemble any Pokémon, Mega Man, any Navi, or any existing character.
- **Style, palette, lighting and outlines:**
  - Pixel art at 2× Game Boy Advance detail: crisp pixels, no anti-aliased blur on edges, a limited palette per object.
  - Dark 1-px outlines on objects. The panels read as glossy metal tiles with a lit top face.
  - The light comes from the attack: the fireball casts warm light on nearby panels.
  - Mood reference: the "MODERN LIGHT" panel of `poc/screens/vision2_concept.png`.
- **Required elements:**
  - the 6×3 grid with red and blue frames;
  - the front edge on the bottom row;
  - the Net sky;
  - two placeholder fighters;
  - the fireball, its burst and its light;
  - the cracked, grass and ice panels;
  - empty HUD frames.
- **Elements to avoid:**
  - any Pokémon, Mega Man or Battle Network character, or any recognisable creature;
  - Nintendo, Game Freak, Capcom or other logos;
  - any readable text or numbers (the engine draws text);
  - perspective that breaks the straight grid;
  - painterly blur.
- **Variant count:** 3, all with the same composition:
  - **A "crisp hi-bit":** hard outlines and flat shading with 2–3 tones.
  - **B "lit hi-bit":** the same pixels, plus soft additive glow and bloom from light sources.
  - **C "BN-faithful":** closest to the Battle Network handheld look, just at double detail.
- **Accepted creative freedom:** colours within the red/blue sides, the sky motif, the effect styling, and the panel
  surface detail.

## Acceptance criteria

**Source-art review** (Khaled picks the variant; the implementation agent checks):

- The grid is 6×3, straight, and in the layout guide's position; red frames are on the left, blue on the right.
- Each panel type is identifiable at a glance.
- Both fighters are original placeholders.
- There is no readable text and no logos.
- The fireball's light visibly affects the nearby panels.

There is no in-game acceptance: this is a concept. The chosen variant becomes the style reference for ASSET-003 and
ASSET-004.
